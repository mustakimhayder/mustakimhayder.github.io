Md. Mustakim Hayder

Graduate Researcher Exploring Ultrafast Laser Applications and Material Behavior

Welcome
Thank you for visiting my profile. I am Md. Mustakim Hayder, a master's student in Mechanical Engineering at Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET), where I also completed my bachelor's degree.Currently, I am a research assistant working under the guidance of Dr. Kazi Arafat Rahman, with a focus on the study of ultrafast laser ablation of metals. My research employs molecular dynamics simulations to understand the fundamental mechanisms of material removal and laser-matter interactions.
I am passionate about exploring how nanoscale material behavior influences performance at larger scales, particularly in extreme environments such as high-intensity laser irradiation and advanced manufacturing processes like additive manufacturing. By integrating computational and experimental approaches, I aim to advance materials science and next-generation manufacturing technologies while addressing real-world challenges in material performance and manufacturing efficiency. I am eager to collaborate on interdisciplinary projects that bridge theoretical research and practical applications,addressing real-world challenges in material performance and manufacturing efficiency.
